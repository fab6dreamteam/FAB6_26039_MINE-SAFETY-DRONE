Mounting schedule Position is a design decision, not a convenience — each row states the constraint that fixes it.
ComponentMounts toOrientationWhy there
MPU6050 front-right arm flat, axes aligned Rigidly bolted — no foam, no gel. This is not the flight IMU; frame vibration is the signal it exists to measure. Damping it would erase the data.
DHT22 front-left arm vents downward 108 mm from the MQ cluster and inside prop-wash. Any closer and the ~1.6 W of heater dissipation biases it by several degrees.
MQ-2 / MQ-4 / MQ-7 pod floor, aft bay sensing face down Needs airflow, so the bay is vented on the floor and both sides. Placed aft and low so its heat rises away from the analog board.
MLX90614 + SG90 pod nose, right sweeps ±60° horizontal Needs unobstructed line of sight past the pod shell and clear of the MQ bodies, which are warm and would sit inside the cone.
HC-SR04 pod nose, centre level, dead ahead Centred so its reading corresponds to the flight path. Level rather than tilted, or it ranges the floor instead of obstacles.
FPV camera pod nose, left 15° down-tilt Standard FPV tilt for forward flight. Electrically isolated from the ESP32 — this is an independent analog chain.
LoRa Ra-02 top plate, aft-left whip vertical Antenna vertical for an omnidirectional pattern in the tunnel plane, and 190 mm from the VTX antenna.
5.8 GHz VTX top plate, aft-right antenna up, cross-pol Maximum separation from the LoRa whip. The risk is the VTX regulator's broadband noise, not the band itself.
ESP32 + signal board pod, mid bay USB toward hatch Reflashing without disassembling the pod. Analog board kept away from the servo's high-current return path.
BEC1 / BEC2 bottom plate beside PDB Short, fat leads to the battery tap. Two separate BECs so the VTX's noise never reaches the sensor rail.
3S LiPo under bottom plate aft of centre Counterweights the forward pod. Slide it fore/aft on the strap to trim the CG after the pod is loaded.
Tether anchor pod belly, at CG trails aft Anchored at the CG so tether tension produces no pitch moment. Anywhere else and the drone fights the cable.

Three build notes that bite later

Extend the landing gear. The stock F450 legs do not clear a belly pod. Either extend them by 30–40 mm or the pod takes the landing load and the MQ sensors take the shock.

Balance the CG after the pod is loaded, not before. Fit everything, then slide the battery on its strap until the airframe balances at the frame centre. A nose-heavy quad burns its rear motors and shortens an already short flight time.

Route the I²C leads to the servo-mounted sensor last, and give them slack. That bundle flexes every scan. Keep it under 150 mm, use flexible 28 AWG silicone, and strain-relieve both ends — a broken I²C line mid-flight silently kills the thermal channel.
